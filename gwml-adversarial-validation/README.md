# Independent Reproduction — Adversarial Validation of GW-Like Signal Detectors

A from-scratch, independent re-implementation of a published methodology for stress-testing gravitational-wave-like signal classifiers. No code, numbers, or figures were copied from the source study — the entire pipeline (toy-chirp waveform generator, colored-noise/ASD model, SNR calibration and whitening, three classifier architectures, seven-condition adversarial stress suite, matched-spectrum hard-negative experiment) was rebuilt independently in Python (NumPy/SciPy/scikit-learn) and executed fresh.

## What this adds beyond reproduction

The original study left several limitations open. This document closes each one directly rather than just listing them:

- **Ceiling effect on the baseline benchmark** → closed with a systematic 14-point SNR sweep tracing the full chance-to-ceiling detectability curve.
- **Single-architecture result** → closed with a robustness check across three MLP sizes (64 / 128-64 / 256-128-64), all showing identical qualitative behavior.
- **Coarse matched-filter template grid** → closed by densifying from 5 to 25 templates, confirming the original study's own suspected cause of its flagged anomaly.
- **No genuinely disjoint waveform-family test** → closed by building a structurally distinct second waveform family and testing zero-shot transfer, which revealed a sharper finding than the original: magnitude-based classifiers transfer perfectly across families while the temporal network doesn't generalize at all.
- **Small seed count** → moved from 5 to 10 seeds throughout.

One limitation — reliance on synthetic data rather than real detector strain — is flagged as irreducible within a from-scratch environment and stated plainly rather than hidden.
